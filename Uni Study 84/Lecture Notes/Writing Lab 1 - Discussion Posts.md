## Requirements
3-4 paragraphs
Grade determined by the effort in it

You bring a unique perspective to the subject you are talking about.

Use first person.

## Strategies
1. Put unique thoughts into post
2. Share something of yourself
3. Include a question in posts when possible (be curious). It is a **discussion** post, so throw the post back and forth. 
4. Spend time editing the post
	1. Speak out loud
5. Avoid content that's off topic
6. Provide meaningful feedback to your classmates
	1. No *I like this post*
	2. Why you like it? 
	3. What do you disagree
	4. What understanding of it do you have? 
	5. Put the name of the person who said it
7. Include information from reading, lectures
	1. Cite them, of course. 
8. Track due dates, and **start early**

## Event Reflection Assignments
What questions might be helpful in framing what to include/write about for the event reflections? 
1. What is key to your experience
2. What did you learn
3. What questions do you still have
4. What inspired you
