# Research
1. Identify a challenge
2. Figure out how to resolve it
3. Resolve it

## Benefits of Research
1. Hands on experience
2. Collaboration
3. Real world applications
4. Apply classroom knowledge
5. Career readiness skills

## Research Models
### Faculty Lab
Part of larger research group
Direct from faculty or grad student
Contributing to a broader research goal
Lower autonomy; faculty directed
### Independent Project
Self -directed
Faculty guidance, but student lead
Exploring a specific academic challenge


# When to start
1. Do I have time
2. Do I want to clarify goals/plans
3. How long do I want to do this
4. How long before I graduate
5. Do your academic requirements affect your research timeline
6. Meet with an **academic advisor**

# How to start
[UROP roadmap](https://urop.uci.edu/research-roadmap/)
Reach out by sending an email with **deep insight to the research topic**.
