# Social Construction
**Social Construction** = Something is real, meaningful or valuable when society tells us it is
1. What we take for granted as real, natural, or self evident is assigned **different meanings** and values in **different cultural contexts**
	1. Now, children are protected. They were not a few hundred years ago. 
2. Human beings act towards ideas, concepts, and values on the basis of the meaning that those things have for them
3. These meanings are a product of social interaction in human society
4. These meanings are modified and filtered through an interpretative process that each individual uses in dealing with outward signs

# Race and Ethnicity
**Race** = a socially defined category based on real or perceived biological differences between groups of people

**Ethnicity** = a socially defined category based on common language, religion, nationality, history or some other cultural factor

**Symbolic Identity** = Expression of group membership

**Situational Identity** = Deliberately assert identity in some situations while downplaying it in others

# Minorities
**Minority group** = a social group that is systemically denied the same access to power and resources available to society’s dominant group

# Racisms
**Racism** = an ideology or set of beliefs about the claimed superiority of one racial or ethnic group over another

**Prejudice** = an idea about the characteristics of a group that is applied to all members of that group and is unlikely to change regardless of the evidence against it

**Discrimination** = **unequal treatment** of individuals based on their membership in a social group, usually **motivated by prejudice**

# Discrimination
**Individual Discrimination** = when one person treats others unfairly because of their race and ethnicity

**Institutional Discrimination** = systematic and widespread, it occurs when institutions practice discriminatory policies that affect whole groups of individuals

# Privilege
**Privilege** = an unlearned advantage accorded to members of dominant social groups

**Colorblind Racism** = an ideology that removes race as an explanation for any form of unequal treatment

**Race Consciousness** = an ideology that acknowledges race as a powerful social construct that shapes individual social experiences

# Cultural Appropriation
**Cultural appropriation** = the adoption of cultural elements belonging to an oppressed group by members of the dominant group, without permission and often for the dominant group’s gain

In other words, **cultural steal**. 

# Theoretical Approaches
## Functionalism
1. Cultural differences and the lack of integration into the larger society on the part of minorities tend to feed fear and hostility
2. Functionalist theories on groups focus on social solidarity and group cohesion

## Conflict
1. Focus on struggle for **power and control**
2. Racism partly *driven* by **economic competition** and struggle over scarce resources (Bonacich)
3. Early racist government policies and individual racism/discrimination led to the creation of a black underclass, however, **economic factors not racial ones now continue to perpetuate that underclass** (Wilson)
4. Racist beliefs become institutionalized and therefore a part political and economic life (Almaguer)

## Symbolic interactionism
1. Race and ethnicity are part of our identity and are displayed as part of our self presentation
2. **Passing** = living as if one is a member of a different racial category
3. **Embodied identities** = those elements of identity that are generated through others’ perceptions of our physical trait
