# The Self
**SELF /SELF-IDENTITY** = *definitions* people hold about *themselves*, which indicate both their unique combination of biopsychosocial characteristics and overall location in the social structure

1. *快乐*原则与*现实*原则
2. “自我”在“本我”的冲动性需求与“超我”的理想化规则之间进行调节。

## Freud: Three Personality Processes
1. **Id** (本我) = biological demands of the organism
2. **Ego** (自我) = learned behaviors which seek to satisfy the demands of the id
3. **Superego** (超我) = demands of society beliefs, values, norms, conscience

## Cooley: Mirrored Self
1. Imagination of our appearance to others
2. Imagination of their judgment of that appearance
3. Self-feeling of pride/mortification based on that imagined judgment

![[Pasted image 20260811141643.png|129]]

**Self** = the capacity to engage in reflexive action
**Significant Others** = significant to the self

In other words, if we *do not interact* with others, we *do not have a self*. 
### Example: wave hands
We wave hands back because *we guess that other would think* wave back means bye. 

### Example: Self esteem
Socialization is guessing what other people think about you. 
Self esteem is merely our *guess of other peoples thoughts* about you. 
However, our ability to guess is *not good*. 
We almost always **underestimate** outselfs, since we almost always **think negatively**. 

## Mead: Stages in the Development of the Self
1. Preliminary Stage
	1. Presymbolic communication
	2. Babies don't speak: they make *noises* that we assign meanings to. We get **overexcited** when hearing babies making *noise*, so babies **make these noises again**.
	3. Understanding meaning through **reactions**. 
	4. Reinforcing the **symbols** through **repetition**. This leads to *Play Stage*. 
![[Pasted image 20260813130814.png|266]]
2. Play Stage
	1. Symbolic communication
	2. Taking the **role** of the other one at a time
		1. Playing catch: switch role between catcher and thrower
		2. Doll game: Taking care of the dolls
	3. Eventually, they **think from others** without acting them out. **Think from others** is the beginning of [[#Cooley: Mirrored Self|Self]]! 
	4. They would also understand that peoples **relationship** are different. 
	5. They understand social **norms** and **consequences**. They develop **abstract thinking**. This leads to *Game Stage*. 
![[Pasted image 20260813130848.png|265]]
3. Game Stage
	1. Taking the role of several others simultaneously
	2. Requires **abstract thinking** of understanding **rules** or **norms**.
	3. View other's conmments towards the generalized "me" as **Self**. 
	4. View internal conversation between "I" and "me" as **Thought** or **Mind**.

# Self-Presentation
How the self is constructed/*presented* in **every day social interactions**. 

The way we present our self to others **varies in different situations**. 

**Dramaturgy** (拟剧论)

## Definition
For a situation to be successful, people must share a common view of the situation

1. People need to agree on a **Frame**
	1. **Frame** (情景): the type of social occasion in which they are participating
	2. 
2. People need to agree on  **Situated Identities** 
	1. **Situated Identities** (情景身份): the identities they will grant one another
	2. Different situations require different identities
3. People need to agree on the roles

## Managing Regions
There's a parallel between the performance's front and back side. 
1. **Front Region** – settings accessible to outsiders where one acts in line with normative expectations. 
2. **Back Region** – settings inaccessible to outsiders where one violates front region performances

## Impression Management
![[Pasted image 20260813142658.png|392]]

**FACE** (面子) = positive social value claimed in social interaction
## Failures in Impression Management: Spoiled Identities
1. **Embarrassment**
	1. *Public identity is challenged* and/or discredited
2. **Spoiled Identities**
	1. Cooling-out
	2. Persuading a person whose *performance is unsuitable* to accept *alternative identity*. 
3. **Identity Degradation**
	1. *Cannot perform* in original role 
	2. Often due to *social ritual*
	3. More severe than losing face

# Life Course Socialization
1. Primary – Secondary Socialization
	1. **Primary**: 创造自我
	2. **Secondary**: 学习角色
2. Adult/Developmental Socialization
	1. **Normative role transitions**: 做该做的事
	2. 入学、就业、结婚、生育、退休
3. Anticipatory Socialization
	1. 提前开始学习该角色的知识、技能和价值观。
4. Reverse Socialization
	1. 本该被教育的人变成了教育别人的人
5. Resocialization
	1. **去社会化**: 剥夺私人空间、严格的作息和惩罚体系
	2. **重塑新我**: 通过严苛的纪律和灌输改造人
	3. 军队、监狱、修道院、戒瘾所
