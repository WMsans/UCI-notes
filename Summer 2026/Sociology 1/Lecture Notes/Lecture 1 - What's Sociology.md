## Macrosociological Perspectives
### Funcitionalism - *The society works like a machine* 
society is conceived as a stable orderly system made up of interrelated parts or structures
the function of each element generates the stability of the whole

1. **Structural funcitonalism**
	1. **Durkheim** demonstrated the effectiveness of the *scientific* method to study social reality
	2. Some of his works include: 
![[Pasted image 20260804142250.png|444]]

Division of Labor is the relationship happened in a industrialization where people get together to work instead of by a similar culture. 
Suicide is a behavior when an indivisual feels detatched. 

2. Contemporary Theorists
	1. **Talcott Parsons** described the functions that structures might play in society
	2. **Robert Merton** identified both manifest (obvious) and latent (less obvious) functions of structures
3. **Pro** of functionalism:
	1. It is an attempt at a universal social theory
	2. Provides sense of stability and order
4. **Con** of functionalism:
	1. Suggests dysfunction produces social change, but not explaining what are why dysfunction. 
	2. Provides little insight into social processes
### Conflict - *阶级斗争*
Society is characterized by **social inequality, conflict, and change**
Society is stratified by **inequalities** (social class, ethnicity, etc). 
> The history of all hitherto existing **society** is the history of **class struggles**. -- Karl Marx

1. Capitalism creates two classes- owners of the means of production (*bourgeoisie*) and workers (*proletariat*)
2. The **social change** is driven by the **revolutions** of the *proletariat*. 

The conflict theory has also evolved: 
![[Pasted image 20260804144557.png|463]]

3. **Pro** of conflict theory
	1. Favors practical actions
	2. Marxist ideas have contributed to social movements throughout history
	3. Addresses both macro and micro issues Does not rely on the assumption that an institutions existence means it is beneficial
4. **Con** of conflict theory
	1. Ignores parts of society that are stable and enduring like **shared values** and **common beliefs**
### Weberian Theory - *Bureaucracy*
modern society was characterized by efficient goal-oriented rule-governed bureaucracies. 

Rationalization
1. The application to economic logic to all spheres of human activity
2. The use of formal rules and regulations in order to maximize efficiency

*Iron Cage* : Modern society is made up of **rigid bureaucratic structures** that control our lives. 

## Microsociological Perspectives
### Symbolic Interactionism
![[Pasted image 20260804145400.png|499]]

*People believes society exist; because of that, people acts like it exist; because of that, society exists.*

> [!EXAMPLE]
> When a group of people say a new word, people would **all say the same** new word with the same new meaning.  
> It is ongoing. It is **symbolic interation**. 

![[Pasted image 20260804150012.png|488]]

Mead: 
	Human development and the *symbols* are a fundamental social process. 
	Language is crucial for the development of self and society
Goffman:
	The self is created in interaction and constantly changing depending on the social context
	**Dramaturgy** is the strategic presentation of the self
	**Ethnomethodology** is an everyday analysis of interaction

1. **Pro** of Symbolic Interactionism
	1. Allows for *human agency*
2. **Con** of Symbolic Interactionism
	1. Sometimes accused of being *apolitical* (supporting the status quo)
	2. Sometimes accuses as being *unscientific*
	3. Very micro focused, often limited to *social psychology*
