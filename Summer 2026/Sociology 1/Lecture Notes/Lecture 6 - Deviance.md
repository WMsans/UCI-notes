## Symbolic Interactionism
1. Differential Association Theory: Sutherland
	1. We learn to be deviant through our interactions with others who break the rules
		1. We don't think a behavior is deviance in our environment, but it may be deviance in other environments. 
2. Labeling Theory: Becher
	1. Deviance is determined by **social context**
		1. 精神病院的人永远无法自证。
	2. Primary deviance
		1. Initial action
		2. E.g. You are ok at math
	3. Secondary deviance
		1. Result from label
		2. E.g. Because you are female, you are bad at math. 
	4. Tertiary deviance - redefining the stigma into something positive
3. Self-fulfilling prophecy
	1. A prediction that causes itself to be there merely by being stated
	2. E.g. Stereotype threat vs. stereotype promise

## Stigma
**Stigma** (污名) is a characteristic widely viewed as an **insurmountable handicap** that prevents competent or male trustworthy behavior
![[Pasted image 20260825133935.png|190]]
It is the opposite of "fresh start"

### Stigma types
1. Physical challenges and deformities
2. Character defects such as dishonesty, or mental illnesses that are dangerous to society and themselves.
3. Racial, sexual or religious beliefs that could contaminate or morally debilitate others.

### Stigma Management
1. Passing
2. Stigmatized persons may try to build relationships around the aspects of their selves that are not discredited.
	1. **Temporary stigma**: The stigmatized person may draw attention to their disability to gain **sympathy** and get attention.
	2. **Permanent stigma**: The stigmatized often try to focus attention on attributes **unrelated to their stigma**.

## Crime and deterrence
1. **Deterrence** (威慑): assumes punishment will have a deterring effect
2. **Retribution** (报应): emphasizes retaliation for the crime as appropriate
3. **Incapacitation** (丧失能力): criminals should be confined to protect society from them
4. **Rehabilitation** (改造): prison system should try to reform the criminal

The criminal justice system reflects the society in which it operates and can replicate its inequalities
