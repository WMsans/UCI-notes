# Midterm Exam Review Questions
## The Sociological Imagination
1. What is the main concern of sociology?
	1. Sociology is the *scientific study of human connection and behavior*. 
	2. It is the science of society. 
	3. It studies how human affects the society, and how society affects human.
2. What is the purpose of sociological theories?
	1. The sociological theories are conclusion and abstract of the observations from society. 
	2. They are our knowledge of society. 
	3. *They are used to predict future of society*. 
3. What are the main theoretical perspectives in sociology?
	1. Macro
		1. *Structural Functionalism*
			1. Observations only
			2. *Society is a complex, interconnected system whose parts work together to promote solidarity, stability, and equilibrium*
		2. Conflict theory
			1. Karl Marx
			2. Class conflict is the source of improvement of society
			3. Led to some of the greatest movements and improvements of the world.
		3. *Weberian Theory* (bureaucracy theory)
			1. Society is a strict institution that manages everyone. 
	2. Micro
		1. Symbolic interactionism
			1. Peoples interactions, thoughts, are all driven by symbols, or ultimately language. 
4. Describe the sociological imagination
	1. The sociological imagination is the ability to think how an individual's behavior is associated with the society/culture/group that he is in. 
5. Be able to list the early theorists in sociology and briefly describe their work
	1. *Comte*
		1. *scientific study of society to guide social progress and order*
	2. *Martineau*
		1. *translated Comte into English*
		2. *Commented US culture in her perspective*
	3. *Spencer*
		1. *Social Darwinism*
	4. *Durkheim*
		1. *Structural Functionalism*
	5. *Marx*
		1. *Conflict Theory*
	6. *Weber*
		1. *Society is efficient, goal-oriented, rule-governed **bureaucracies***
6. Be able to distinguish between micro and macro sociology
	1. Micro studies interactions between individuals
	2. Macro studies the larger institutions in scale of society and nations. 
7. Discuss the similarities and differences between functionalism, conflict, and symbolic interaction theories.
8. Discuss the strengths and weaknesses of each of the three theoretical perspectives.
	1. Functionalism
		1. Pro: first comprehensive sociology theory
		2. Con: Bad at explaining changes and dysfunction
	2. Conflict theory
		1. Pro: good at explaining changes and dysfunction. 
		2. Con: *Partly ignores stable parts like shared beliefs*
	3. Symbolic interactionism
		1. Pro: *human agency* (treats the individual and the social at the same level of analysis)
		2. Con: apolitical, unscientific, limited. 
9. Be able to apply each perspective to an example.
	1. Functionalism
		1. People go to clubs. Club leaders are well educated and elected. Club members are respecting each other. People collaborate and share knowledge to reach an equilibrium. 
	2. Conflict Theory
		1. Worker produces more value than he earns. His life becomes very bad since all the product are exploited away from him. He goes ahead and joined the honorable revolution to liberate his class.  
	3. Symbolic interactionism
		1. We talk with language.
