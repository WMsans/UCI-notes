## Curiosity
When doing something, be curious about why. 
When talking to/doing things for professors, be curious about why. 
## Failure
Making mistakes are critical to learning. 
It is a sign of *pushing* yourself, and *pushing* yourself is important. 

>[!warning] No More High School
>In high school, GPA is everything. No mistake is worth making. 
>In college, taking risks and exploring is the priority. 

Go to clubs. 
**Go and connect with faculty**. 
	Asking about test during office hour is an *annoying* question
	Asking about a academic concept is a *great* question. 
# QnA
## How to pick up from failures
Failures can feel bad
*Just get use to it*.

Some failures you can learn from, while others you can just regret. 
## Tricky Exams
If you feel it that way, remember the feeling of that moment. 
And remind yourself of that feeling when you are in class, in review, and in study. 
## Tinker around
Do practical projects with your knowledge. 
If doing physics, make simulation programs.
If doing computer science, train some mini models.
