# Panethnicity
(泛文化)
## Historical Cause
### Before 1950
all Asian Americans are either Chinese, Japanese, or Philippine. 
Almost all are laborers. 
The cause is racism. 
1. Chinese exclusion Act
2. WWII and Jap Internment camps

### After 1960
After the baby boom, the **second gen** Chinese, Japanese and Philippine American becomes the force in **Asian American Movement**. 

1. End of exclusion
2. Linguistic and cultural differences blur
3. Changing residential patterns
4. Increasing intercultural communication

### Construction of panethnicity
1. The purpose is to gain political power. 
2. It is influenceed by idea that "Asians are colonized in America"
3. It is alliance intra-racially and inter-racially

### Asian American Movements

2. New left movement

	2. Originated but differed from Marxism
3. Women's movement
