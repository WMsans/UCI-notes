# 阅读笔记 7.2 —— 后记忆的两种"写法"：Nam Le 的"族裔故事"伦理，与 lê thi diem thúy 的"阁楼里的祖父母"

## 阅读文献

1. **Le, Nam. "Love and Honor and Pity and Pride and Compassion and Sacrifice."** 载 *The Boat*，**3–28 页**。纽约：Alfred A. Knopf，2008 年。（**全书首篇**，也是被选入 *Best American Short Stories 2007* 的那一篇；扫描件共 15 页，含书页 3–28，**末页另附下一篇《Cartagena》的首页**，可略过）
2. **lê thi diem thúy. *The Gangster We Are All Looking For*.** 纽约：Anchor Books（Random House），2003 年。**78–99 页**。（扫描件共 13 页：版权页＋题词页＋**书页 78–99**；即 7.1 中 Espiritu 反复引用的那一部分——**"战争没有开始也没有结束……它像一只裂开的船，挤满人，唱着悲伤的歌，越过海洋"** 就出自 87 页）

> **本单元解码**：7.1 给的是**理论**（Hirsch 的后记忆、Espiritu 的双向沉默与"用成绩偿付悲伤"、Vang 的范畴史）；7.2 把理论撤掉，只给**两个文学文本**——而且是一对极讲究的对照：
> **Nam Le 写的是"能不能写"**：一个在爱荷华写作班里的越南裔作家，被同学、老师、经纪人、女友围住，逼他交出"族裔故事"；他交出来了，**父亲把它烧了**。
> **lê thi diem thúy 写的是"怎么记"**：一个小女孩用一张祖父母的照片、一句"你的阿姨叫什么名字"、一根埋在沙里的铁管、一面变成纸板的"长城"，把父母的战争和自己被炸开的出生拼成一个家；**最后她把这个家塞进阁楼，够不到的地方**。
> **一句话串两篇**：**Nam Le 问"谁有权把父母的苦写成故事、又卖给谁"——他的答案是一次烧稿；lê 问"在没有档案的地方，一个孩子拿什么记得"——她的答案是照片、身体、和一句"血只在我的喉咙里"。**
> **一个贯穿两篇的问题**：**父亲的过去，究竟是"遗产"还是"素材"？** Le 的叙述者把它当素材（标上 "ETHNIC STORY" 四个大写字母）；lê 的叙述者把它当遗产（塞进阁楼，"你不需要看见或触摸他们，才知道他们在"）。

## 关键定义（务必记牢）

| 术语                                       | 定义                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **postmemory（后记忆，Hirsch）**               | **复习 7.1**：**与正在被记忆的事件在时间与空间上相隔，却与目击者的记忆一同生活**；**它不是"记忆"，而是"想象、投射、创造"，但"在情感强度上近似于记忆"**；**它"不是一个身份位置，而是一个嵌在多种中介形式之中的、代际传递的结构"。** **7.2 的用处**：**两个文本都是"后记忆"的成品**——Le 写的是**后记忆被市场化的那一刻**，lê 写的是**后记忆被一个孩子的想象组装出来的过程**。                                                                                                                                                         |
| **"ethnic lit"／"ethnic story"**          | **1990 年代以降美国出版与写作班里的一个市场范畴**：**以族裔出身、移民经验、异国饮食与创伤为卖点的文学**。**Le 的故事里它被三种声音定义**：**写作课老师说"Ethnic literature's hot. And important too."**；**同学说"It's a license to bore."、"I'm sick of ethnic lit. It's full of descriptions of exotic food."**；**经纪人说"Your background and life experience."** **考点**：**这三句话加起来就是一套完整的"少数族裔作家生产链"——它不否认你的痛苦，它只是给痛苦定了价。**                        |
| **"the old verities"（Faulkner 的"古老真理"）** | **标题的出处**。**福克纳 1950 年诺贝尔奖演说**：**作家应当写"心中的古老真理……缺了它们，任何故事都短命而注定毁灭：love and honor and pity and pride and compassion and sacrifice（爱、荣誉、怜悯、骄傲、同情与牺牲）"**。**Le 的用法极刻薄**：**同学把福克纳的六项美德当成"老生常谈（the old verities）"来推销，却在同一段话里劝他"你就写越南船民嘛"**——**即：抽象的普世价值可以谈，具体的族裔素材必须交。**                                                                                                          |
| **"the gangster"（流氓）**                   | **小说标题的核心词，在文本里至少四层意思**：① **字面上，父亲是"从北方来的佛教徒流氓（a Buddhist gangster from the North）"**——**一个家里"满是做梦的人"的、有贼有赌徒有酒鬼的家族**；② **母亲骂人的话——"别用你那双流氓的手碰我"**；③ **父亲自己喊出来的话——"让我看看那个流氓！让我看看他的手！"**——**他把"流氓"从骂名变成一个要求：看看我这双做过事的手**；④ **女儿结尾的宣言——"我长大了要做我们都在找的那个流氓（the gangster we are all looking for）"**。**考点**：**这是一个"被污名占为己有"的词，与 3.1／3.2 的"model minority／yellow peril"是一套逻辑的反面使用。** |
| **nước**                                 | **题词**：**"在越南语里，'战争'与'国家、国土、家园'是同一个词：nước。"** → **整个文本的语法前提**：**在这本书里，打仗和想家是同一件事，无法分开叙述。** **加分点**：**nước 同时是"水"**——**这解释了全书为什么到处是海、船、雨、水罐、鱼缸、"把海折成一个蓝色的方块"。**                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **"a wayward archive"（任性的档案，Espiritu）**  | **7.2 的文本正好是这个词的实物版**：**一张祖父母的照片、一部老好莱坞电影式的公寓楼梯、一张写着 "Tom's Professional Gardening Service" 的名片、一个误称所有难民小孩为 "Yang" 的年鉴**。**Espiritu 的判词**（复习）：**越南难民的后代没有"纪念的基础设施"，只有"一个任性的档案、对不可言说之物的抓取、与鬼魂的对话"。**                                                                                                                                                                         |

| **"differential inclusion"（差别化纳入）**      | **Le**：**叙述者拿到的是"被纳入但被差别化"的写作位置——你的稿子可以进写作班、可以卖六位数，但前提是它得是"族裔的"**；**lê**：**一家人住进 1940 年代的 Navy Housing 平房、必须签一张"保证不把鱼骨丢进垃圾处理器"的表格**——**被接纳的条件是先被规训。**                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **"racial lumping"（种族笼统化）**              | **7.1 里是"巴士上的老人说 My brother died in your jungle"**；**7.2 里是年鉴里的一句**：**"他们叫我们 Yang，因为有一年老挝小孩里有一批姓 Yang 的来了我们学校。海军住宅区的小孩就开始管所有难民小孩叫 Yang。"** → **考点：这是"种族笼统化"最经济的一个文学证据——一个姓，吞掉越南人、柬埔寨人、老挝人、苗人。**                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **"the attic"（阁楼）**                      | **lê 的核心意象**：**"把祖父母搬进来"**——**母亲把女儿举起来，女儿把装着照片的信封推进阁楼的缝隙，"推到手指够不到的地方"**；**结尾拆屋时，母亲在废墟里喊"Ma／Ba"**——**"他们坐在阁楼里，坐得像王族一样，在黑暗里发亮"。** → **阁楼＝**"**记忆的正式居所**"：**它不被看、不被摸，但它必须存在（Hirsch：后记忆靠"没有索引性的联结"运作）。**                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **"the tube"（校舍前的铁管）**                   | **lê 的出生意象**：**校舍前一根来历不明的长铁管，"孩子们躲在里面、从里面爬过、坐在上面交换秘密"，盖着防水布，"像一间没有墙的房间里的、烧黑了的原木"**。**母亲临产时看它，把它看成"一个被埋在沙里的、烧得很惨的巨人的手臂"**——**"她分不清他是被埋了想出来，还是想把自己埋起来却没来得及把手臂盖好。来不及做什么？"** **考点**：**这根管子同时是**"**儿童的游戏场／孕妇进不去的容器／战争的残肢／棺材**"——**是全书写得最好的一处"多义物"。**                                                                                                                             |
| **"the Great Wall of China"（长城）**        | **父亲为女儿在后院斜坡上修的一条小路／土墙**：**"像一条河，从 Crandall Drive 陡坡的顶端蜿蜒到略微弯曲的底部"**。**被驱逐的那一夜，女儿在路灯下看见它**——**"我看见长城。在路灯的光里，它只是长长的一条纸板。"** → **考点：全篇最锋利的一次"祛魅"——父亲的爱、童年的奇观、与一个非法改建的斜坡，是同一个东西。**                                                                                                                                                                                             |

## 一、Nam Le（2008）—— "你要不要写越南？"：一个族裔故事的诞生与焚毁

### 一句话概括
这是一个**关于"能不能写"的故事，而不是关于"越南"的故事**。二十五岁的叙述者（越南出生、澳洲长大、做过律师、现在爱荷华写作班最后一年）被三股力量同时挤压：**写作班的同学说"你就写越南船民嘛，族裔文学正热"；女友 Linda 说"你在替他找借口"；父亲 Ba 飞来美国，什么也不说，只是在他的公寓里洗碗、扫地、把腰疼当成一场表演**。他终于在截止前两夜交出了那篇东西——**在纸上打下 "ETHNIC STORY" 四个大写字母，写下父亲十四岁在美莱（My Lai）看到的那条排水沟**。**父亲的回应不是感动，是把稿子烧了。** 于是全篇真正的题目浮出来：**当"他们的过去"变成"我的材料"，写作是一种纪念、一种背叛，还是一门生意？**

### 人物与情境（考 identification 够用）
- **叙述者 "Nam"**：**名字是父亲给的**——**"他给我取了他所放弃的那片国土的名字"**（Nam＝越南的"南"）。**前律师**（墨尔本，穿会磨破皮肤的衣服，一杯白水，六分钟一个单位的时间）；**现在写小说，写了一年多只写了三个半故事、一万七千字**。
- **Ba（父亲）**：**南越军人**，1975 年后**在再教育营待了三年**，**1979 年组织全家出逃**（那年他二十五岁）；**与妻子分开后独居悉尼，失业，给三四十年前的朋友发长邮件**；**腰坏了（"神经液在脊椎之间漏出来"，带了 CAT 片子来给儿子看）**；**穿着借来的、大得不合身的羽绒服**。
- **Ma（母亲）**：**在纺织厂做工，学会用锁边机，一直给儿子寄钱**；**父亲"发现她在支持我"之后下了最后通牒**。
- **Linda**：**美国女友**；**"她脸上有一种关闭的表情"**；**她提出那个叙述者答不出的问题**（见第 6 节）。
- **写作班的朋友／老师／经纪人**：**族裔文学市场的三个人格化声音**（见第 2 节）。
- **桥下的流浪汉**：**一个"裹得严严实实、留着胡子"的人，在一只燃烧的汽油桶上取暖**——**全篇的道德转折点**。

### 0. 开篇：雨、打字机、与一首"最好的诗"
> **"My father arrived on a rainy morning. I was dreaming about a poem, the dull thluck thluck of a typewriter's keys punching out the letters. It was a good poem—perhaps the best I'd ever written."**（3 页）

- **醒来时诗已经散了**：**"The fields are glass…"** 只剩下一行；**"然后 tum-ti-ti，一个扬抑抑格，行末，然后是下一行的 excuse 与 alloy。"** **现实替换了诗**：**"风大的、雨打过的停车场；一间几乎被我的床占满的暗房；我父亲小小的身体，把水滴滴在硬木地板上。"**
- **"He had a habit of speaking in Vietnamese proverbs. I had long since learned to ignore it."**（4 页）**父亲的第一句谚语**：**"A day lived, a sea of knowledge earned."（活一天，赚一片海的知识。）**
- **第一处"阶级"细节**：**父亲在他自己的公寓里巡视**——**"Very fancy… You even have a piano."**（5 页）**然后是那句**：**"I knew you'd never really quit."**（我知道你从来没真的放弃。）**叙述者认出了那种笑**：**"某种东西在他脸后动了一下，我发现自己又坐回了那张高脚凳上，手指追着节拍器，前后、前后——"**
- **为什么这三行重要（考点）**：**父亲的"失望"不是道德指责，是**"**你既没有当成律师，也没有当成作家**"这一条**双重的败绩**；**而这份失败感，正是后面"我要写一个能卖的故事"的全部动力。**

### 1. 时机：一篇三天后要交的稿子，和一个"最糟的时刻"
> **"THE TRUTH WAS, he'd come at the worst possible time. I was in my last year at the Iowa Writers' Workshop; it was late November, and my final story for the semester was due in three days."**（6 页）

- **"It was no wonder I was drinking so much."**
- **他在父亲来之前一夜才告诉 Linda**；**而 Linda 的身体场景写得极不客气**（**"我们本可以是黑暗里的任何两个声音"**）——**这是全篇第一次把"身体的亲密"与"叙述的不可靠"并置**。
- **叙述者把父亲藏起来的方式很具体**：**把四分之三瓶的尊尼获加塞到书桌下、用杂志盖住烟灰、把马桶刷洗干净、把 Linda 的照片面朝下盖住**。**"我工作在这个房间里，也睡在这个房间里。"**（5 页）**→ 一个把"写作"和"生活"塞进同一个房间的人，正在准备把父亲也塞进去。**

### 2. "Ethnic literature's hot"：市场是怎么说话的（本节考试密度最高）
- **触发句**（朋友在派对后、路灯下）：**"Writer's block? … How can you have writer's block? Just write a story about Vietnam."**（9 页）
- **背景案例（考点：他是被"成功样本"逼出来的）**：**写作班"最近最成功的一位"——一个想移民美国的中国女性，写了一本"关于处于移民各阶段的中国人物"的短篇集，"含蓄而好"**；**传闻她拿到了六位数的两本书合约**。**"当然，这是所有人都在谈的事。"**
- **三种市场声音（务必能背）**：
  1. **写作课老师，在酒吧**：**"It's hot… Ethnic literature's hot. And important too."**
  2. **两位来访的经纪人**：**"There's a lot of polished writing around… You have to ask yourself, what makes me stand out?"** ／ **"Your background and life experience."**（**"像念咒一样慢"**）
  3. **同学**：**"I'm sick of ethnic lit… It's full of descriptions of exotic food."** ／ **"You can't tell if the language is spare because the author intended it that way, or because he didn't have the vocab."** ／ **"It's a license to bore."**（**"这是一张允许你无聊的许可证。"**）
- **"装束"的讽刺（必记）**：**他听说"朋友的朋友"——一个华盛顿特区来的哈佛毕业生，"为了书的封面照，穿着传统的尼日利亚长袍摆姿势"**。**叙述者的反应是一组画面**：**"我想象自己站在一片稻田里，戴着一顶草编的斗笠。然后我想象我父亲在同一片田里，穿着他那身磨破了的军装，年轻，眼神很硬。"** **→ 这一句是全篇最短的一次"伦理地震"：族裔文学的"布景"与父亲真实的战场，是同一片田。**
- **朋友那段"文学批评"**（10 页，注意它其实是**最高级的自我批评**）：**"The characters are always flat, generic. As long as a Chinese writer writes about Chinese people, or a Peruvian writer about Peruvians, or a Russian writer about Russians…"** **接着**：**"As long as there's an interesting image or metaphor once in every this much text"**——**他用拇指和食指比出半页**——**然后停住，"嘴里转出一个怀疑的鬼脸。我看得出他为某件事生气。"** **叙述者把话岔开**：**"Look… Those guys have guns."**（**门廊上两个小孩拿着假木枪**）
- **"老真理"的出场（标题出处，必背）**：
  > **"Faulkner, you know… he said we should write about the old verities. Love and honor and pity and pride and compassion and sacrifice."**（**然后是一声"像巨型打字机锤下来"的爆响**）
  > **"I know I'm a bad person for saying this… but that's why I don't mind your work, Nam. Because you could just write about Vietnamese boat people all the time. Like in your third story."**
- **叙述者的反应（考点：这是他"卖"的第一动机）**：
  > **"Catalogued like that, under the bourbon stink of his breath, my stories sank into unflattering relief."**
  - **然后是"幻想杀童"那一段**（10–11 页）：**"我要把他们的故事从医院的床上对着麦克风讲出来。我要在县监狱里写我的故事。我要杀死他们中的一个，也许是意外，然后永远不谈这件事，对任何人。"** **——"我的牛仔裤上没有洞。"**
  - **→ 这是全篇对"素材伦理"最狠的一次自嘲**：**一个作家最诚实的时刻，是他想象用别人的血来写完自己的句子的时候。**

### 3. 父亲的纪律：一具被规训出来的身体
- **家规（清单式记忆，考点）**：**"No personal phone calls. No female friends. No extracurricular reading."** **小学时父亲让他列一张"暑假每天十小时的学习时间表"，偏离就罚。**
- **体罚的精确度**：**"He knew how to cane me twenty times and leave only one black-red welt, like a brand mark across my buttocks. Afterward, as he rubbed Tiger Balm on the wound, I would cry in anger at myself for crying."**（13 页）
- **榴莲那一段**：**"有一次我母亲说漏了嘴，说我闻到榴莲就吐，他就逼我在客人面前吃下去。Đói ăn muối cũng ngon. 饿的时候，盐也是好吃的。"**
- **"父亲被虚弱吸引，尽管他容不下我身上的任何虚弱"**：**"He was a soldier, he said once, as if that explained everything."**（13 页）
- **父亲的历史课（第一层：反美）**：**"After what the Americans did? How could you fight on their side?"** —— **"'I had nothing but hate in me,' he said, 'but I had enough for everyone.'"**（25 页）**→ 这是全篇最漂亮的一句"政治句"：恨不需要理由，只需要配额。**

### 4. 桥上：流浪汉、"We are Vietnamese boat people"、与"Welcome to America"
- **场景**（13 页）：**父子过桥散步**（**父亲说要去"那条河"，不是美术馆**）；**叙述者买了两杯咖啡回来，父亲已经下到河岸，站在一个裹得严严实实、留着胡子的人旁边，那人正俯在一只燃烧的汽油桶上**。
- **对话（全篇的道德核心，必背）**：
  > **"This is my son," my father said… "The writer."**
  > **"Would you like some coffee?"** → **"Thank you, no."**
  > **"I read his story," my father went on in his lilting English, "about Vietnamese boat people."** **He gazed at the man, straight into his blank, rheumy eyes, then said, as though delivering a punch line, "We are Vietnamese boat people."**
  > **"Welcome to America," the man said through his beard. He didn't look up as I closed my fist around the damp bills.**
- **这句为什么是全篇的枢纽（必写）**：**父亲把自己的身份——"越南船民"——像**一句**punch line 一样交给了一个美国流浪汉**。**这不是自怜，是**一次**翻译**：**把"我的历史"翻译成"美国能听懂的最低共同单位——无家可归"。** **而流浪汉的回答（"欢迎来美国"）完美地接住了这个翻译——他不必知道越南在哪，他只需要知道你也睡在外面。**
- **"我攥紧那几张湿钞票"**：**叙述者的位置被这一场彻底暴露——**他是那个**付钱的人**（**替父亲给钱**），也**是那个把父亲写下来的人**。

### 5. 父亲的历史课（第二层）：美莱、酷刑的名字、与"三年前"
- **十四岁那年的发现（考点）**：
  > **"When I was fourteen, I discovered that he had been involved in a massacre. Later, I would come across photos and transcripts and books; but that night, at a family friend's party in suburban Melbourne, it was just another story in a circle of drunken men."**（14 页）
- **那场讲述（全篇最长的一次直接引语，14–17 页）**：**一桌越南男人、报纸铺地、蓝塑料布、"Fuck your mother! 怎么这么久"、"给他五瓶"**；**父亲坐在圆圈边缘，脸喝得通红，"像晒伤了一样"，用筷子夹起血冻（tiết canh）**；**他讲：**
  - **"Heavens, the gunships came first, rockets and M60s. You remember that sound, no? Like you were deaf."**；**母亲、四个姐妹、面包师 Tran 太太躲在地窖里**；**"If we stayed there, the Americans would think we were Viet Cong."**
  - **直升机的数量、"他们让我们跪在水里"、"No VC no VC"**、**一个"脸很胖的美国兵"一边装弹匣一边哭**；
  - **水沟里"路上有尸体，一个婴儿只剩下半个头"、"一个和尚，僧袍正在变粉"**；
  - **祖父 Long 双手合十行传统礼，GI 用枪托敲他的头、转枪、把刺刀滑进他的喉咙**；**母亲捂住叙述者的眼睛**；**父亲说"我看见他把枪的保险从连发拨到单发"**；
  - **最后**：**"When they started shooting I felt my mother's body jumping on top of mine; it kept jumping for a long time… and everything was dark and wet and warm and sweet."**
- **叙述者的注脚（方法论，极考点）**：
  > **"Maybe he didn't tell it exactly that way. Maybe I'm filling in the gaps. But you're not under oath when writing a eulogy, and this is close enough."**（17 页）
  - **→ 这一句是 7.2 对 Hirsch 的直接呼应**：**后记忆的工作不是"核证"，而是"补上缺口"；而"写悼词不必宣誓"——这是它全部的合法性，也是它全部的危险。**
- **地理的落点（必背的一行）**：
  > **"He grew up in the province of Quang Ngai, in the hamlet of Tu Cung, later known to the Americans as My Lai. He was fourteen years old."**
  - **→ 全篇最重要的一次"坐标换算"**：**父亲的村庄，在美国的记忆里有一个专有名词（My Lai），而这个词的意思是"美军屠杀"**——**所以父亲既是那场屠杀的目击者，也（后来）是南越军队的士兵：加害者／受害者／旁观者三种位置在他一个人身上重叠。** **这正是 Hirsch 警告的"家庭会把受害者与加害者的差别抹掉"的一个具体个案。**
- **"再教育营"清单（考点：把"创伤"写成"技术名词"）**：**"the tiger-cage cells and connex boxes, the different names for different forms of torture: the honda, the airplane, the auto."**
  > **"They tie you by your thumbs, one arm over the shoulder, the other pulled around the front of the body. Or they stretch out your legs and tie your middle fingers to your big toes—"**
  - **父亲示范给他看**："一个瘦削的老头摆出密宗式的姿势"，**"隐约有点可笑"**；**然后"在'汽车'那一式里他缩了一下，脸上突然绽出笑，叫我扶他坐到泡沫床垫上"**。
  - **→ 这是全篇最残忍的一次"日常化"**：**酷刑被讲成了瑜伽动作，而叙述者的反应是"我等得不耐烦"**——**"我是个坏儿子。"**（22 页）

### 6. Linda：三个问题，没有一个能答
- **她翻开稿子读了两页**：**"I don't get it… What are you doing?"** → **"You never told me any of this."** → **"Did he tell you this? Now he's talking to you?"**
- **她真正的判词（全篇最痛的一句）**：
  > **"I think you're making excuses for him."**（**"你在替他找借口。"**）
  > **"You're romanticizing his past… to make sense of the things you said he did to you."**
  > **"You said he abused you."**
  - **叙述者的反应**：**"这些字太多了，还有与它们相连的东西。……'我从没说过。'"**
- **她走之前的规则**：**"不带着一个爱的表示离开一场争吵"**——**她亲了他的头顶**。**"我没有看她。"**
- **→ 考点：Linda 这个人物不是"不理解亚洲家庭的美国女友"，她是**"**后记忆的第二个读者**"**：**她要求一个"能被道德判断的版本"，而叙述者要给的是"能被卖掉的版本"。** **两者都不是父亲的版本。**

### 7. 打字机、"ETHNIC STORY"、与"四十五页的笔记"
- **写作的物质条件（考点：这些细节都是关于"生产"的）**：**"我买了一台古董店的电动 Smith Corona。插上电，它嗡嗡响得像一只热带水族箱。它搁在桌上很好看。"** **"为了灵感，我读极其正式的维多利亚诗歌，喝纯苏格兰威士忌。"** **"这世界上的事不是一直都在发生吗？我要做的只是记录下来。"**
- **决定性的一夜（17 页）**：
  > **"LATE THAT NIGHT, I plugged in the Smith Corona… I grabbed the bottle of Scotch from under the desk and poured myself a double. Fuck it, I thought. I had two and a half days left. I would write the ethnic story of my Vietnamese father. It was a good story. It was a fucking great story."**
  > **"I fed in a sheet of blank paper. At the top of the page, I typed 'ETHNIC STORY' in capital letters. I pushed the carriage return and scrolled down to the next line. The sound of helicopters—ters in a dark sky. The keys hammered the page."**
  - **→ "ETHNIC STORY" 四个大写字母是全篇的题眼**：**它不是标题，是**标签**——**他先贴标签，再填内容**。**而"直升机的声音"作为打字声出现，意味着：他已经学会了把战争当成音效。**
- **父亲的"读到错处"**：**"There were mistakes in your last story too."** —— **叙述者胃里一沉**（22 页）。**父亲在教他"事实"层面的错误（Rạch Giá 的那场戏、登船之前），儿子在心里想"它们是故事，是小说"。**
  > **"They're stories," I said, chewing casually. "Fiction."**
  > **He paused for a moment, then said, "Okay, Son."**
  - **→ 这一小段是全篇最含蓄的一次"谴责"：父亲接受了"小说"这个说法，然后**用烧稿回应了它**。**

### 8. 父亲阻止他写的那一次谈话（本节是"伦理"的核心，最可能出 short essay）
> **"'Why do you want to write this story?' my father asked me."**
> **"'It's a good story.'"**
> **"'But there are so many things you could write about.'"**
> **"'This is important, Ba. It's important that people know.'"**
> **"'You want their pity.'"**
> **"I didn't know whether it was a question. I was offended. 'I want them to remember,' I said."**
> **"He was silent for a long time. Then he said, 'Only you'll remember. I'll remember. They will read and clap their hands and forget.' For once, he was not smiling. 'Sometimes it's better to forget, no?'"**
> **"'I'll write it anyway,' I said."**（24–25 页）

- **接着是那句把一切说穿的话**：
  > **"'If I write a true story,' I told my father, 'I'll have a better chance of selling it.'"**
  > **"He looked at me a while, searchingly, seeing something in my face as though for the first time. Finally he said, in a measured voice, 'I'll tell you.' … 'But believe me, it's not something you'll be able to write.'"**
- **父亲的三个反驳，逐一记住**：
  1. **"You want their pity."**（**你要的是他们的怜悯**——把"纪念"还原成"情感交易"）
  2. **"They will read and clap their hands and forget."**（**读者会读、会鼓掌、会忘**——否认"被记住"的可行性）
  3. **"Sometimes it's better to forget, no?"**（**有时候忘掉更好，不是吗**——一句 7.1 里"don't ask, don't tell"的父方版本）
- **父亲笑了（全篇唯一一次真正的笑，必背）**：
  > **"His face opened up and he began to laugh, without self-pity or slyness, laughing in full-bodied breaths. I was shocked. I hadn't heard him laugh like this for as long as I could remember. Without fully knowing why, I started laughing too. His throat was humming in Vietnamese, 'Yes… yes… yes,' his eyes shining, smiling. 'All right. All right. But tomorrow.'"**
  - **然后是那句**：**"'I need to think,' he said."** 以及**两句谚语的对打**：
    > **"My son a writer. Có thực mới vực được đạo."**（**有饭吃才谈得上修道**——"空肚子能把你拖多远"）
    > **"'Một người làm quan, cả họ được nhờ.'"**（**一人做官，全家沾光**）**→ "一个学者是全亲戚的福气。他惊讶地看了我一眼，然后大笑，用力点头。这一句我攒了好几年。"**
  - **→ 考点：这是全篇最温柔也最残忍的一段——**父亲用谚语说的是"你会饿死"，儿子用谚语回的是"我会光宗耀祖"；**两个人说的是同一件生意。**

### 9. 下午的采访：四十五页笔记与父亲的“一世”
- **采访的场景（26 页）**：**餐桌两端、黄色便笺本、父亲说话、儿子记笔记**；**"午后的光从窗户进来，把他的身体框在一个银色的方格里，慢慢沉向他的脚边，把一切弄暗"**；**"他讲到高峰时段的街道，讲到晚到深夜"**；**电话响了两次，他把电话从插座上拔掉。**
- **父亲讲的内容清单（考点：这是他"终于开口"，而儿子在"记录"）**：**童年、家庭、美莱**；**被抓丁进南越军队**；**遇见母亲、婚礼、1975 年西贡陷落**；**再教育营的强迫自白、灌输、饥饿**；**"毁了他背的日常劳作"**；**"随意的处决"**；**虎笼与 connex 箱**。
- **"一个比抱怨更大、比记忆更危险的过去"**：
  > **"A past larger than complaint, more perilous than memory."**（27 页）
- **叙述者的工作方式（方法论，考点）**：**"我把笔记仔细读了一遍，四十五页。我把两天前写的那版草稿又读了一遍。然后我把它们都放到一边，重新开始打字，再也不看它们。"**
- **幻想中的"父子相认"（必背，因为它是被推翻的）**：
  > **"He would read it, with his book-learned English, and he would recognize himself in a new way. He would recognize me. He would see how powerful was his experience, how valuable his suffering—how I had made it speak for more than itself. He would be pleased with me."**（27 页）
- **现实**：**"HE WASN'T IN THE APARTMENT when I woke up."** 桌上有一张纸条：
  > **"I am going for a walk. I have taken your story to read."**
- **他骑车满城找他**（**雪、结冰的路、College Green 上被冻脆的草、Washington Street 上一阵风把榆树的叶子剥下来，"厚而慢、无声地落下"**）。

### 10. 烧稿（全篇高潮，必背）
> **"I was halfway across the bridge when I saw him. I stopped. He was on the riverbank… He stood with the tramp, both of them staring into the blazing gasoline drum. The smoke was thick, particulate. For a second I stopped breathing. I knew with sick certainty what he had done. The ashes, given body by the wind, floated away from me down the river. He patted the man on the shoulder, reached into his back pocket and slipped some money into those large, newly mittened hands. He started up the bank then, and saw me. I was so full of wanting I thought it would flood my heart. His hands were empty."**（28 页）

- **然后是全篇最难的三个句子**：
  > **"If I had known then what I knew later, I wouldn't have said the things I did. I wouldn't have told him he didn't understand—for clearly, he did. I wouldn't have told him that what he had done was unforgivable. That I wished he had never come, or that he was no father to me."**
  > **"But I hadn't known, and, as I waited, feeling the wind change, all I saw was a man coming toward me in a ridiculously oversized jacket, rubbing his black-sooted hands, stepping through the smoke with its flecks and flame-tinged eddies, who had destroyed himself, yet again, in my name."**
  > **"The river was behind him. The wind was full of acid."**
- **结尾的河的比喻（全篇最后一句，必背）**：
  > **"And it occurred to me then how it took hours, sometimes days, for the surface of a river to freeze over—to hold in its skin the perfect and crystalline world—and how that world could be shattered by a small stone dropped like a single syllable."**
- **怎么读这三处"考点"**：
  1. **"我后来才知道的事"（what I knew later）被刻意留白**——**全篇最关键的空白**：**父亲烧的是儿子的稿子，还是儿子抄下的那四十五页笔记？还是他只想在火边取暖、顺手烧掉？文本不回答。** **而"His hands were empty"承接的正是 7.1 里 lê 的那双"做过事／没能做事"的手**（**两个文本以"手"互相照亮，见第三节**）。
  2. **"又一次以我的名义毁掉自己"**：**"in my name" 一语双关**——**父亲给儿子取的名字，就是他被放弃的国土**；**所以儿子写父亲，等于父亲又一次以儿子的名义把自己交出去。**
  3. **河的比喻在说什么**：**后记忆就像河面结冰**——**它需要几个小时、有时几天，才能把"一个完美而水晶般的世界"收在自己的皮里；而这个世界可以被"一颗像单个音节一样落下的小石头"击碎**。**→ 这是全篇对"写作"最终的判决：**文章就是那颗小石头；它既能击碎冰面，也是唯一让冰面显形的东西。**
- **"a single syllable" 与开篇的呼应（加分点）**：**开篇是打字机把字母"打"出来（"the dull thluck thluck of a typewriter's keys punching out the letters"）**，**结尾是"一个音节"落下把世界击碎**——**首尾都是"一个声音改变一个世界"，只是方向相反。**

### 11. 与 7.1 理论的三个接口（写 essay 直接用）
1. **Hirsch 的"表演性索引"**：**父亲的过去在文本里没有一张照片可依**（**唯一的"图像"是叙述者自己拼的"稻田里的斗笠"和"穿破军装的父亲"**）——**所以这篇小说是"纯粹的后记忆"：没有索引，只有需要。**
2. **Hirsch 的"家庭会把历史私有化"**：**儿子把父亲的村庄（Tu Cung）替换成一个美国能识别的名词（My Lai），把一条排水沟替换成一篇可卖的故事**——**这正是"私有化"的技术细节。**
3. **Espiritu 的"好难民叙事"**：**写作班／经纪人／"族裔文学正热"，就是"好难民叙事"在生产端的样子**：**你的痛苦受欢迎，条件是它得好看、得"exotic"、得"once in every half page 有一个好意象"。**

## 二、lê thi diem thúy（2003）—— "把祖父母搬进阁楼"：一个孩子如何组装一场战争

### 一句话概括
这 22 页是**"后记忆"最完整的一次现场演示**。叙述者（小女孩，1978 年六岁随父出逃，母亲与妹妹 1980 年才到）**没有亲历父母的战争**，却要把它讲出来——**她的材料只有五样：一张祖父母的照片、母亲讲过的恋爱故事、出生那一夜的传说、圣地亚哥 Linda Vista 的街区，和自己的身体**。**于是全书的方法就是：把"照片里看不见的东西"（被赶出家门）、"故事里没法核证的东西"（北方贵族、法国母亲、长第二个脚趾）、"国家不给的东西"（历史课、赔偿、尊严）全部用比喻补上**。**而结尾——拆迁、铁丝网、"把海折成一个蓝色的方块"——把这个家整个搬进阁楼：够不到，但知道它在。**

### 人物与情境
- **叙述者**：**无名的小女孩**（母亲叫她 "Child"）；**全书唯一的"我"，也是唯一的"讲述者"**——**"血只在我的喉咙里，就在这里，在我讲这一切给你听的地方。"**
- **Ba／Anh Minh（父亲）**：**北方来的"佛教徒流氓"**；**南越军人**；**在再教育营待过**；**在 Linda Vista "一夜之间"成了园丁**（**买下叔叔 Twelve 的一车工具和一盒名片，"Tom's Professional Gardening Service"**）；**赌钱、酗酒（周一夜橄榄球）、在屋顶上滚着哭**。
- **Ma／em My（母亲）**：**南方天主教女学生**；**因为要嫁给一个北方佛教徒流氓，被父亲用她从小扫地的那把扫帚打出家门**；**在 Linda Vista 剃光头、戴反戴的蓝棒球帽**；**"像男人一样"但仍是母亲，"我会凭她的手和她的走法认出她"。**
- **哥哥**：**留在越南**——**"如果天和海能跟着我们来这里，人为什么不能？"**（母亲不回答）
- **Anh（"the chicken egg girl"）**：隔壁邻居，**每天早上推着小车卖鸡蛋**；**她家后院的蓝防水布"是南中国海那种明亮的蓝"。**
- **祖父母**：**照片里坐在竹椅上的一对老人**；**"搬进阁楼"之后，成为这个家唯一"在场而不可见"的成员。**

### 0. 题词：nước
> **"In Vietnamese, the word for war and the word for a nation, a country, and a homeland are one and the same: nước."**

- **→ 这一个词决定了全书的句法**：**凡是讲"我们怎么逃出来的"，都同时是"我们家在哪里"；凡是讲"国家"，都同时是"水"。** **考点：能说出 nước 的三重义（战争／国族／水），就能解释全书为什么到处是海、船、雨、鱼缸、"把海折成一个蓝色的方块"。**

### 1. 照片里的越南：一张"看不见"的决裂
> **"Vietnam is a black-and-white photograph of my grandparents sitting in bamboo chairs in their front courtyard. They are sitting tall and proud, surrounded by chickens and a rooster… My grandfather's broad forehead is shining. So too are my grandmother's famous sad eyes. The animals are oblivious, pecking at the ground. This looks like a wedding portrait though it is actually a photograph my grandparents had taken late in life, for their children, especially for my mother."**（78 页）
> **"When I think of this portrait of my grandparents in their last years, I always envision a beginning. To or toward what, I don't know, but always a beginning."**

- **紧接着，同一张照片里"看不见"的内容（全篇方法论的第一句，必背）**：
  > **"When my mother, a Catholic schoolgirl from the South, decided to marry my father, a Buddhist gangster from the North, her parents disowned her. This is in the photograph, though it is not visible to the eye. If it were, it would be a deep impression across the soft dirt of my grandparents' courtyard. Her father chased her out of the house, beating her with the same broom she had used every day of her life, from the time she could stand up and sweep until that very morning that she was chased away."**
- **→ 这就是 Hirsch 的"照片是屏幕"的文学实证**：**照片只给"图像的、你可以投射的表面"，而"真相在别处"**；**叙述者做的事，正是把"照片里看不见的那道凹痕"重新写出来。**
- **"这看起来像一张结婚照，其实是晚年拍的"**：**一张为子女拍的、预备着"以后看"的照片**——**它一开始就是"给还没发生的事"用的**（**照片的功能是"以后的记忆"，不是"当时的记录"**）。

### 2. 父母的恋爱：一个"可以是个故事"的过去
- **"那年有好几个年轻男人在她家干活、替她父亲跑腿、和她母亲一起摘菜。所有人都明白这些人是来向她求婚的。我母亲说她完全没有这个理解。"**（79 页）**"她把他们当兄弟，有时当叔叔。"**
- **"Ma says love came to her in a dark movie theater."**——**"她不记得是什么电影、也不记得为什么去，只记得她一个人去，发现自己坐在他旁边。在黑暗里她看不清他的脸，只觉得他的侧影好看。她想他知不知道她正用眼角看他。"
- **"在他身上看，没有尴尬也没有羞耻，只有一种奇怪的求知欲：她想用指尖描摹他的轮廓，直到把每一处都记住，好在一年中最黑的那几个夜里、在搁浅的渔船影子里，把他叫出来。"
- **"恋爱的初期，我母亲讲故事。她招供出精心编织的、关于战争结束的梦：要吃的东西（一张宴会桌、芒果堆到天花板）；她要唱、要跳、要把头发像马鬃一样甩过头顶；要从一只脚跳到另一只脚。作为家里最受宠的、负责任的女儿／姐姐，她不是这样的；跟我父亲在一起，在森林里，我母亲变得放肆，醉在自己的年轻与爱的可能性里。她丢下家里的活计，把裤脚卷到膝盖，把光脚踩进水坑，学会了抽烟。"**（80 页）
- **"她给头发系上一条朱红的丝带。"**
- **森林里的那一夜（考点：一句对话就把"婚姻"说成"被骗"与"自愿"的混合物）**：
  > **"One evening, she followed my father in circles through the forest, supposedly in search of the clearing that would take them to his aunt's house. They wandered in darkness, never finding the clearing much less the aunt she knew he never had."**
  > **"'You're not from here,' she said."**
  > **"'I know.'"**
  > **"'So tell me, what's your aunt's name?'"**
  > **"'Xuân.'"**
  > **"'Spring?'"**
  > **"'Yes.'"**
  > **"She laughed. I can't be here, she thought."**
  > **"'My father will be looking for me—'"**
  > **"'It's not too late. I'll walk you home.'"**
  > **"In the dark, she could feel his hand extending toward her, filling the space between them. They had not touched once the entire evening and now he stood offering his hand to her. She stared at him for a long time. There was a small scar on his chin, curved like her fingernail. It was too dark to see this. She realized she had memorized his face."**（81–82 页）
- **"我的第一个关于父亲脸的记忆，被南越一个军营的盘绕铁丝网框住"（82 页）**——**母亲的声音穿过铁丝网，低声叫他的名字**：
  > **"'Anh Minh, Anh Minh.' His name becomes a tree she presses her body against. The calling blows around them like a warm breeze and when she utters her own name, it is the second half of a verse that begins with his. She drops her name like a pebble into a well. She wants to be engulfed by him, 'Anh Minh, em My. Anh Minh, em My.'"**
  > **"The barbed wire gates open and she crosses through to him. She arrives warm, the slightest film of sweat on her bare arms. To his disbelieving eyes she says, 'It's me, it's me.'"**
  > **"I trail behind them, the tip of their dragon's tail. I am drawn along, like a silken banner on the body of a kite."**
- **"我第一个关于父亲脸的记忆"这句话的双重性（考点）**：**它说的是"记忆"，其实是"想象"**——**叙述者不在场；她是从母亲的故事里、从"铁丝网"这个道具里，搭出父亲的第一个形象。** **这就是后记忆的字面定义。**
- **石头与脚趾（全篇最好的"以物代人"，必记）**：
  > **"'For a handful of pebbles and my father's sharp profile, my mother left home and never truly returned. Picture a handful of pebbles. Imagine the casual way he tossed them at her as she was walking home from school with her girlfriends. He did this because he liked her. Boys are dumb that way, my mother told me. A handful of pebbles, to be thrown in anger, in desperation, in joy. My father threw them in love. Ma says they touched her like warm kisses, these pebbles he had been holding in the sun.'"**
  > **"'What my father told her could have been a story. There was no one in the South to confirm the details of his life.'"**
  - **父亲的自我叙述（"半贵族北方家庭"、"母亲是法国人，是父亲养的众多情妇之一"、"十六岁那年跑出家门、来到南方"）** ／ **"他解开靴子，把脚抽出来，让她仔细看他第二个脚趾比别处长得多。'这是贵族的明确标志。'"**
  - **"我见过小偷、赌徒、酒鬼，他们让我想起我家里的人。因为他们都是做梦的人。我家是一座满是做梦者的花园，他们仰面躺着、望着天，喝醉了，被自己的梦噎住。"**
  - **母亲的回答（全篇最好笑、也最冷的一记）**：
    > **"'What does it mean if your second toe is longer than your other toes?'"**
    > **"'It means… your mother will die before your father,' her mother said."**
    > **"'I heard somewhere it's a sign of aristocracy.'"**
    > **"'Huh!'"**
- **脚趾被母亲改写成"船"（必背的意象）**：
  > **"When my mother looked at my father's bare feet she saw ten fishing boats, two groups of five. Within each group, the second boat ventured ahead, leading the others. She would climb a tree, stand gripping the branch with her own toes and stare down at his… she imagined what she saw to be ten small boats surrounded by black water, a fleet of junks journeying in the dark."**（84 页）
  - **→ 考点：这是全书的"翻译法则"——**父亲的欺骗（关于贵族血统）被母亲的想象力改写成一首关于逃亡的诗**。**"十个脚趾＝十艘渔船"：连他撒谎的痕迹，她也拿来做船。**

### 3. 出生：铁管、烧焦的巨人、磷光女孩
- **出生（84–86 页，全篇最密集的一段，必读三遍）**：
  - **"我生在我祖父母家后面的巷子里。凌晨三点，我母亲从她和父亲婚后住的那间小屋的床上爬起来。我父亲不在，在打仗。Ma 最小的妹妹来和她住，帮她带我哥哥，那时还是个婴儿。Ma 把两个人留在吊床里睡，我哥哥躺在我姨妈的臂弯里，然后一个人走了出去。"**
  - **"她在沙滩上划出一条弯曲的线。动作一阵一阵的，像浪里被抛的球，她好像被弹着走，没有方向。"**（**这句写的是宫缩，也是全书的节奏**）
  - **"她走到校舍，坐在它前面的地上，靠着头一级台阶。她感到沙粒压在她的背上。每一粒都是一个细微的针刺，疼越来越深。很快她觉得背要裂开，血在涌。"** **"我想，我要流血死掉了。她把手放在肚子上。我们要死了。"**
  - **铁管**：**"校舍前面横着一根长长的金属管。没有人知道它是从哪里来的。它好像一直在那儿。孩子们躲在里面，从里面爬过，在两端互相说话，从它上面列队走过，坐在它上面、靠在它旁边交换秘密。课间可以玩的东西太少了。这根长铁管就成了一切。一块防水布撑在它上面，替它挡太阳。这根管子看起来像一间没有墙的房间里的、烧黑了的原木。孩子们一排坐在管子上，说话时头这样那样地点着，看起来像是坐在一只搭了篷的筏子上。"**
  - **"我出生的那一夜，我母亲看着那根管子，想象它是一个被埋在沙里的、烧得很惨的巨人的手臂。她分不清他是被埋了、正想出来，还是他想把自己埋进沙里，却没来得及把手臂盖好。来不及做什么？她听过邻镇一个女孩的故事：那个女孩在一次凝固汽油弹轰炸里死了。轰炸发生在一个特别热的夜里，女孩走到海边把脚泡进水里。他们发现她漂在海上。汽油弹的磷让她的身体发亮，像一盏灯笼。在我母亲心里，她为这个女孩搭了一个篷。她开始哭，想着那个被埋的巨人、那个漂着的女孩，这些停在半路上的身体，正在去某个地方的路上。"**
  - **"她开始朝那根管子走。她突然很想钻进去。世界让她觉得危险，而她一个人。"** **"在管子口她弯下腰，她的肚子堵住了口。她试了另一头，另一个口。又是她的肚子挡住了她。'可我记得，'她出声地嘟囔，'我小时候有时在里面睡。' 这就是她现在想要的，睡在管子里。'Tall noses come from somewhere—' 'Not from here.' 'Not tall noses.'"**
  - **→ 这段为什么是全章的理论核心（考点）**：**铁管同时是**"**儿童的游戏场／孕妇进不去的容器／战争留下的残肢／棺材／她小时候的床**"——**四种时空（童年、战争、临产、死亡）在一根管子上叠合**。**这就是后记忆的"结构"：不是线性传下来的故事，而是**一个可以被反复投射的物**（Hirsch 的"屏幕"）。**
- **"眼睛"那一段（87 页，写"种族化的目光"，最考点）**：
  > **"Eyes insinuate, moving from her nose to mine then back again. Mouths suck in air, color it into the darkest shade of contempt, then spit it at her feet as she walks by. I am riding on her hip. I am the new branch that makes the tree bend but she walks with her head held high. She knows where she pulled me from. No blue eye."**
  - **"No blue eye" 是全篇最锋利的一句**：**在一个把"蓝眼睛"当作价值尺度的国家里，母女俩的证据是彼此。**
- **"战争是一只折断翅膀的鸟"（87 页，7.1 已引，必背全段）**：
  > **"Ma says war is a bird with a broken wing flying over the countryside, trailing blood and burying crops in sorrow. If something grows in spite of this, it is both a curse and a miracle. When I was born, she cried to know that it was war I was breathing in, and she could never shake it out of me. Ma says war makes it dangerous to breathe, though she knows you die if you don't. She says she could have thrown me against the wall, until I broke or coughed up this war that is killing us all… but didn't I know? War has no beginning and no end. It crosses oceans like a splintered boat filled with people singing a sad song."**

### 4. Linda Vista：黄房子、鸡蛋女孩、"Yang"、鱼骨表格
- **地理的三级下降（必背，是全书的"地址诗"）**：
  > **"We live in the country of California, the province of San Diego, the village of Linda Vista."**
- **国家在场的第一个证据（考点：Vang 的"难民是国家要解决的问题"的日常版）**：
  > **"We live in old Navy Housing bungalows built in the 1940s. Since the 1980s, these bungalows house Vietnamese, Cambodian, and Laotian refugees from the Vietnam War. When we moved in, we had to sign a form promising not to put fish bones in the garbage disposal."**（88 页）
- **街区（必背）**：**"一栋单层的木板灰泥房……我们这排房子和另一栋单层房之间夹着六栋两层房。隔着一条棕色土路，对面又是一排黄房子，和我们一样，像一只悲伤的孪生兄弟看着我们。Linda Vista 到处是这样的房子，涂着剥落的橄榄绿、婴儿蓝和晒成日光黄的颜料。"**
- **"Yang"（种族笼统化的文学实证，必背）**：
  > **"At school their kids are Most Popular, Most Beautiful, Most Likely to Succeed. Though there are more Vietnamese, Cambodian, and Laotian kids at the school, in the yearbook we are not the most of anything. They call us Yang because one year a bunch of Laotian kids with the last name Yang came to our school. The Navy Housing kids started calling all the refugee kids 'Yang.'"**
  > **"Yang. Yang. Yang."**（89 页）
  - **→ 对应 7.1 Espiritu 的 "racial lumping"**：**一个姓，取代了越南人、柬埔寨人、老挝人、苗人；"年鉴里我们不是任何项目里的最多"——被统计，但不被区分。**
- **"天和海跟着我们来"（全篇最动人的一句母亲的话，配女儿的追问）**：
  > **"Ma says living next to Anh's family reminds her of Vietnam because the blue tarp suspended above Anh's backyard is the bright blue of the South China Sea. Ma says, isn't it funny how sky and sea follow you from place to place as if they too were traveling."**
  > **"Thinking of my older brother, who was still in Vietnam, I ask Ma, 'If the sky and the sea can follow us here, why can't people?'"**
  > **"Ma ignores my question…"**
- **父亲"一夜之间"成了园丁（阶级与"好难民"叙事的细节）**：**"Ba becomes a gardener. Overnight. He buys a truck full of equipment and a box of business cards from Uncle Twelve, who is moving to Texas to become a fisherman. The business cards read 'Tom's Professional Gardening Service' and have a small green picture embossed on them, a man pushing a lawn mower. The man has his back to you, so no one holding the card can tell it's not Ba, no one who doesn't already know."**（89–90 页）
  - **"He says I can be his secretary because I speak the best English."** → **接电话的那段自我介绍（必背）**：**"Hello, you have reached Tom's Professional Gardening Service. We are not here right now, but if you leave us a message, we will get back to you as soon as possible. Thank you."**
  - **→ 考点：**"Tom" 是一个借来的名字，"背对着你的那个人"是一个借来的形象——**这就是 7.1 里 Espiritu 的"用公开的成绩偿付私人的悲伤"，在最微小尺度上的样子：一张名片，一个英文口音，一个秘书。**
- **"谁看见过这些？"（90 页，全篇最漂亮的一次反问，必背）**：
  > **"It is hot and dusty where we live. Some people think it's dirty but they don't know much about us. They haven't seen our gardens full of lemongrass, mint, cilantro, and basil. Driving by with their windows rolled up, they've only seen the pigeons pecking at day-old rice and the skinny cats and dogs sitting in the skinny shade of skinny trees. Have they seen the berries that we pick, that turn our lips and fingertips red? How about the small staircase Ba built from our bedroom window to the backyard so I would have a shortcut to the clothesline? How about the Great Wall of China that snakes like a river from the top of the steep hill off Crandall Drive to the slightly curving bottom? Who has seen this?"**
- **绿色公寓（"老好莱坞电影"的比喻，与 7.1 Hirsch 的"预先形成的形象"直接呼应）**：**"我们每次进门都得把门带上。门重重地哐一声，我想象楼上下有一大堆眼睛，从微微拉开的窗帘后面盯着我。院子里四角种着四棵棕榈树，中央一道楼梯，上窄下宽。台阶上铺着假草，像一部老好莱坞电影的布景——那种由一位上了年纪的美人主演、她醒来发现有什么事大错特错的电影。"**
- **搬去 Linda Vista 的理由（黑人／族裔城市空间与暴力的转喻）**：**"有天晚上我们打开电视，听说我们的管理员和他兄弟把一个女人砍成几块、装进十加仑垃圾袋扔进太平洋，袋子冲上了岸。Ma 说她不想住在一个被谋杀的女人作祟的地方。所以我们搬到 Linda Vista，她说那里有很多像我们一样的越南人——那些人唯一的罪不过是赌一点钱、嘬鱼骨头、大声笑、大声吵架。"**（91 页）

### 5. 剃头、照片、与"流氓的手"
- **母亲剃头（考点：把"疯"从病理改写成抗议）**：
  > **"Ma shaved her head in Linda Vista because she got mad at Ba for gambling away her money and getting drunk every week during Monday Night Football. Ba gave her a blue baseball cap to wear until her hair grew back and she wore it backward, like a real badass."**
  > **"After that, some people in Linda Vista said that Ma was crazy and Ba was crazy for staying with her. But what do some people know?"**
- **那封"从越南来的信"（全篇的情绪爆点，必背）**：
  > **"When the photograph came, Ma and Ba got into a fight. Ba threw the fish tank out the front door and Ma broke all the dishes. They said they never should've got together. Ma's sister sent her the photograph from Vietnam. It came in a stiff envelope. There was nothing else inside, as if anything more would be pointless. Ma held the photograph in her hands. She started to cry. 'Child,' she sobbed, over and over again. She wasn't talking about me. She was talking about herself."**（92 页）
  - **→ "Child" 的双关是全章最狠的一笔**：**母亲哭着叫的不是女儿，是"她自己"——被她父亲用扫帚赶出家门的那个女孩。** **一张照片把三十年的距离压成一个称呼。**
- **父亲的回应与"手"（与 Nam Le 的"His hands were empty"互文，必背）**：
  > **"Ba said, 'Don't cry. Your parents have forgiven you.'"**
  > **"Ma kept crying anyway and told him not to touch her with his gangster hands. Ba clenched his hands into tight fists and punched the walls."**
  > **"'What hands?! What hands?!' he yelled. 'Let me see the gangster! Let me see his hands!'"**
  > **"I see his hands punch hands punch blood."**
- **母亲砸碗（把家暴写成一种"仪式"）**：**"Ma 在厨房里。她把纱窗扯下来。她在用人行道给碗碟、盘子、杯子、饭碗打句点。她把它们像鸟一样送出去，在天空里滑翔，没有特定的地方可去。直到它们撞碎。然后她满意地'哈！'一声呼出一口气。"**
  - **"我在走廊里大口喘气。我把碎裂和流血吸进来。"** **"Ba 把手伸进水族箱，我闻出水里那一丝淡淡的血。"** **"Ba 把水族箱从前门扔出去，喊'让我看看那个流氓！'的时候，我把漏出来的水喝掉，把那些漂亮的热带鱼整条吞下去，让它们鲜亮的颜色滑过我的舌头，趁它们还没落到地上、还没在土里把自己埋起来、只剩眼白在太阳下眨。"**
  - **"所有的手都在我的喉咙里，在碎碗碟上割伤自己；鱼在打转，满眼是血，什么也看不见。"**
- **"我长大了要做我们都在找的那个流氓"**（93 页，全书的标题句，必背）：
  > **"When I grow up I am going to be the gangster we are all looking for."**
- **对着邻居小孩的那一场（把"被看"改写成"看回去"）**：
  > **"'What're you looking at?' I ask."**
  > **"'Lookin' at you,' one boy says, half giggling."**
  > **"'Well,' I say, with my hand on my hip, my head cocked to one side, 'I'm looking at you too,' and I give him my evil one-eyed look, focusing all my energy into my left eye. I stare at him hard as if my eye is a bullet and he can be dead."**
  - **→ 与 87 页的 "No blue eye" 首尾成对：**母亲用"抬起下巴"顶住目光，女儿用"一只眼睛当子弹"回击。**这就是这本书的"抵抗语法"——没有宣言，只有姿势。**

### 6. 阁楼：把祖父母搬进来（全篇的"后记忆"定义，必背）
> **"Ma is sitting in the window frame… 'Let me lift you into the attic.'"**
> **"'Why?'"**
> **"'We have to move your grandparents in.'"**
> **"I don't really know what she is talking about, but I say O.K. anyway."**
> **"We have never needed the attic for anything. In fact, we have never gone up there. When we moved my grandparents in, Ma simply lifted me up and I pushed open the attic door with one hand while, with the other, I slipped the stiff envelope with the photograph of my grandparents into the crawl space above. I pushed the envelope the length of my arm and down to my fingertips. I pushed it so far it was beyond reach. Ma said that was all right; they had come to live with us, and sometimes you don't need to see or touch people to know they're there."**（94 页）

- **→ 这一段的三个考点**：
  1. **"搬进来"是比喻，动作是真的**：**没有仪式、没有牌位、没有坟——只有一个信封，一次"推到够不到的地方"。** **这是"没有纪念基础设施"的人的做法（Espiritu 的"任性的档案"）。**
  2. **"you don't need to see or touch people to know they're there"** ＝ **Hirsch 的"后记忆"最简洁的一句定义**：**联结不靠索引性，靠需要。**
  3. **阁楼是"记忆的正式居所"**：**它不被使用（"我们从来不需要阁楼"）、不被进入，但它必须存在**——**结尾拆迁时，阁楼是唯一"还在"的地方。**
- **屋顶上滚动的两个人（95 页，全篇最美的比喻，必背）**：
  > **"Ba came home drunk that night and asked to borrow my blanket. I heard him climbing the tree in the backyard. It took him a long time. He kept missing the wooden blocks that run up the tree like a ladder… Ba put those blocks in for me, so I could win at hide-and-go-seek."**
  > **"When Ba had finally made it onto the roof, he lay down over my room and I could hear him rolling across my ceiling. Rolling and crying. I was scared he would roll off the edge and kill himself, so I went to wake Ma. She was already awake. She said it would be a good thing if he rolled off. But later I heard someone climb the tree, and all night two bodies rolled across my ceiling. Slowly and firmly they pressed against my sleep, the Catholic schoolgirl and the Buddhist gangster, two dogs chasing each other's tails. They have been running like this for so long, they have become one dog, one tail."**
- **"我会凭她的手和她的走法认出她"（95–96 页，母女关系的定义）**：
  > **"Without any hair and looking like a man, my mother is still my mother… Her hands always bring me up, her big peasant hands with the flat, wide nails, wide like her nose and just as expressive. I will know her by her hands and by her walk, at once slow and urgent, the walk of a woman going to market with her goods bound securely to her side. Even walking empty-handed, my mother's gait suggests invisible bundles whose contents no one but she can reveal. And if I never see her again, I will know my mother by the smell of the sea salt and the prints of my own bare feet crossing sand, running to and away from, to and away from, family."**

### 7. 驱逐：铁丝网、"为什么我们总是这样离开？"、与"把海折成一个蓝色的方块"
- **驱逐通知（考点：法律与文盲的对照）**：
  > **"When the eviction notice came, we didn't believe it so we threw it away… The notice also said that if we didn't get out in time, all our possessions would be confiscated in accordance with some section of a law book or manual we were supposed to have known about but had never seen."**（96 页）
- **铁丝网**：
  > **"The fence is tall, silver, and see-through. Chain-link, it rattles when you shake it and wobbles when you lean against it. It circles our block like a bad dream. It is not funny like the clothesline whose flying shirts and empty pants suggest human birds and vanishing acts. This fence presses sharply against your brain. We three stand still as posts. Looking at it, then at one another—this side and that—out of the corners of our eyes. What are we thinking?"**
- **夜里的搬家（"我们像跨越大陆的人一样从窗户滚出来"）**：**"我们把十加仑垃圾袋装满：衣服、锅碗、人字拖、圣母马利亚的瓷像、木佛、中国渔夫灯……我们像跨越大陆的人一样从窗户滚出来。我们在穿越时间，被沉重的家具和一袋袋宝贝压着。"**
- **母亲的崩溃（全章最强的一段独白，必背）**：
  > **"In the car, Ma starts to cry. 'What about the sea?' she asks. 'What about the garden?' Ba says we can come back in the morning and dig up the stalks of lemongrass and fold the sea into a blue square. Ma is sobbing. She is beating the dashboard with her fists. 'I want to know,' she says, 'I want to know, I want to know… who is doing this to us?' Hiccupping she says, 'I want to know, why—why there's always a fence. Why there's always someone on the outside wanting someone… something on the inside and between them… this… sharp fence. Why are we always leaving like this?'"**
  > **"'Take me back!' she says. 'I can't go with you. I've forgotten my mother and father. I can't believe… Anh Minh, we've left them to die. Take me back.'"**
  - **母亲的四个问题就是整本书的政治学**：**谁在干这件事？为什么总有围栏？为什么总有人在围栏外想要围栏里的什么？为什么我们总是这样离开？**
  - **"把海折成一个蓝色的方块"（fold the sea into a blue square）**：**父亲唯一能给她的安慰——**把海变成一个可以带走的、可以折起来的东西**。**这句话是全书的"离散"定义：**你能带走的只有"蓝色的方块"。**
- **三个叔叔的嘲弄（族内对"创伤"的不耐烦）**：
  > **"'My, are you going to walk back to Vietnam?'"**
  > **"'Yeah, are you going to walk home to your parents' house?'"**
  - **→ 考点：这是 7.1 里"1.5 代 vs. 第一代"分裂的另一个版本：**在逃难这件事上，"谁比谁更苦"从来不是一致的。**
- **"长城只是纸板"（全篇最锋利的一次祛魅，必背）**：
  > **"Ba puts his foot on the gas pedal. Our car jerks forward, and then plunges down the Crandall Drive hill. Ma says, 'I need air, water…' I roll the window down. She puts her head in her hands. She keeps crying, 'Child.' Outside, I see the Great Wall of China. In the glare of the streetlamps, it is just a long strip of cardboard."**（98 页）
- **早晨：平整的世界**：
  > **"In the morning, the world is flat. Westinghouse Street is lying down like a jagged brushstroke of sun-burnt yellow. There is a big sign within the fence that reads COMING SOON: CONDOMINIUMS TOWN HOUSES FAMILY HOMES. Below these words is a copy of a watercolor drawing of a large pink complex."**
- **结尾（99 页，全篇最后一段，必背）**：
  > **"We stand on the edge of the chain-link fence, sniffing the air for the scent of lemongrass, scanning this flat world for our blue sea. A wrecking ball dances madly through our house. Everything has burst wide open and sunk down low. Then I hear her calling them. She is whispering, 'Ma/Ba, Ma/Ba.' The whole world is two butterfly wings rubbing against my ear."**
  > **"Listen… they are sitting in the attic, sitting like royalty. Shining in the dark, buried by a wrecking ball. Paper fragments floating across the surface of the sea."**
  > **"There is not a trace of blood anywhere except here, in my throat, where I am telling you all this."**
- **→ 结尾的三个考点**：
  1. **"他们坐在阁楼里，坐得像王族一样"**：**阁楼没有被拆到——因为阁楼里只有一张照片（"纸片漂在海上"）**。**物质的家被拆了，"搬进来的祖父母"还在。**
  2. **"纸片漂在海上"**：**与题词 nước、与全书所有的海、与"十个脚趾＝十艘渔船"闭环**——**照片变成了船。**
  3. **"血只在我的喉咙里，就在这里，在我讲这一切给你听的地方"**：**全书的暴力最终被收进"讲述"这个动作里**——**这是 7.2 的"后记忆"宣言：**血不在现场，血在喉咙；不在过去，在讲述的此刻。** 与 Nam Le 的"一个音节落下把冰面击碎"是同一件事。**

## 三、两篇如何对话

| | **Nam Le（2008）** | **lê thi diem thúy（2003）** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **文体** | **短篇（高度自传性：越南出生、澳洲长大、爱荷华写作班）** | **小说（半自传：1978 年六岁出逃、圣地亚哥 Linda Vista）** |

| **核心冲突** | **能不能写、为谁写、卖给谁** | **怎么记、拿什么记、记在哪里** |
| **父亲** | **南越军人、再教育营三年、1979 组织出逃、腰坏了、独居悉尼** | **北方"佛教徒流氓"、南越军人、再教育营、Linda Vista 园丁 "Tom"、赌钱酗酒** |
| **母亲** | **背景人物（纺织厂、寄钱、最后通牒）** | **核心（被扫帚赶出门、剃头、砸碗、抱着照片哭"Child"）** |

| **"手"的意象** | **"His hands were empty."**（父亲烧完稿子、把手伸进流浪汉手里塞钱） | **"Let me see the gangster! Let me see his hands!"** / **"All the hands are in my throat."** |
| **沉默** | **"We just don't talk about that kind of stuff."** ／ 父亲：**"那不是你能写出来的东西。"** | **父亲从不说战争；母亲用砸碗代替说话；女儿用吞鱼代替说话** |
| **国家** | **以"市场"的形式在场**（族裔文学、经纪人、写作班、"六位数合约"） | **以"表格、通知、铁丝网、推土机"的形式在场**（鱼骨表、驱逐通知、COMING SOON 的粉色楼盘） |
| **结局** | **稿子被烧；"我后来知道的事"留白** | **家被拆；祖父母在阁楼里"像王族一样"** |
| **对后记忆的贡献** | **后记忆的"生产端"：它如何被定价、如何被写坏、如何被拒绝** | **后记忆的"组装端"：它如何用照片、传说、身体与比喻被造出来** |

**三句话串起来（背这个就能写 short essay）**：
1. **Nam Le 把"族裔故事"的生产线拆开给人看**：**老师说它热、经纪人说它看"背景与人生经验"、同学说它是"允许你无聊的许可证"**——**于是叙述者先在纸上打下 "ETHNIC STORY" 四个大写字母，才写第一个句子**。**父亲的回答不是抗议，是把它烧了。**
2. **lê 把"后记忆"的组装过程拆开给人看**：**一张"看不见决裂"的合影、一个"可以是个故事"的恋爱、一根烧焦的巨人手臂般的铁管、一个被所有难民小孩共用的姓（Yang）、一面被路灯照成纸板的长城**——**她的材料全是二手的、假的、别处来的，但每一件都被她用得像真的。**
3. **两个文本合起来，正好是 7.1 那道题的答案**：**Hirsch 说后记忆靠"家庭、照片、公共档案"三种媒介传递，代价是可能把历史私有化**——**Le 演示了"私有化"最彻底的一种形式（把它卖出去）；lê 演示了"私有化"最温柔的一种形式（把它放进阁楼）。**

## 快速记忆口诀

- **一句话套全篇**：**Nam Le 写"族裔故事"怎么被生产出来、又怎么被父亲烧掉；lê 写"祖父母的照片"怎么被推进阁楼、又怎么在拆屋时变成漂在海上的纸片。**
- **Le 的三句话**：**"Just write a story about Vietnam."（市场）→ "You want their pity."（父亲）→ "ETHNIC STORY"（儿子）。**
- **Le 的三个声音**：**老师（热）／经纪人（背景与人生经验）／同学（允许你无聊的许可证）**。

- **Le 的核心比喻**：**河面结冰＝后记忆**；**一颗像音节一样落下的小石头＝文章**。
- **lê 的五个物件**：**祖父母的合影（看不见的决裂）／十根脚趾（十艘渔船）／校舍前的铁管（烧焦的巨人的手臂）／长城（纸板）／阁楼（记忆的正式居所）**。

## Test tips（考点提示）

> 本单元是**两个文学文本**，题型大概率是 **identifications（认人、认物、认句）＋ short essay**。**文学文本不考情节复述，考的是"这个细节能证明哪一个论点"。** **务必能说出：作者、书名、主论点、以及两个可引用的具体细节。**

1. **两篇的基本信息**：**Nam Le（1978 年生于越南，在澳洲长大），《The Boat》（2008）首篇，书页 3–28**；**lê thi diem thúy，《The Gangster We Are All Looking For》（2003，Anchor Books），7.2 选的是书页 78–99**。**加分点：能说出后者的题词是 nước（战争／国族／水同词）。**
2. **Le 的情节骨架（六步）**：**父亲雨天抵达爱荷华（33 小时、三年前见过一面）→ 写作班三天后交稿、一年只写了三个半故事 → 派对上被劝"写越南"→ 桥边父亲对流浪汉说 "We are Vietnamese boat people" → 父亲讲美莱、讲酷刑、讲再教育营（四十五页笔记）→ 父亲烧稿，儿子在河边看见空手。**
3. **"ETHNIC STORY" 那四个大写字母**：**能解释为什么 "He fed in a sheet of blank paper… typed 'ETHNIC STORY' in capital letters" 是全篇的题眼**——**它把"标题"与"标签"合并，把"文学"变成"品类"**。**易错点**：**不要说"他为了钱出卖父亲"**；文本更狠：**他先承认"真实的故事更好卖"，然后父亲用"有时候忘掉更好"回答他。**
4. **"You want their pity" 三种反驳**：能背**"他们要的是怜悯／他们会读、会鼓掌、会忘／有时候忘掉更好"**，**并能指出这三句分别否定了"纪念""读者""记忆本身"**——**这是全篇对"写作能否承担见证"最系统的怀疑。**
5. **"His hands were empty" 怎么读**：**它是全篇最后一句"人物特写"，也是与 lê 互文的枢纽**；**能指出"手"在 Le 里是"劳动、暴怒、给钱、烧纸"的器官，在 lê 里是"打人、抓鱼、砸墙、被展示"的器官。**
6. **"I knew with sick certainty what he had done" 的留白**：**能说明文本没有确证父亲烧的是什么**（**稿子？四十五页笔记？还是只是火堆里的东西？**），**而"我后来才知道的事"被刻意不写**——**这种留白正是"后记忆"的运作方式：证据不足，情感充足。**
7. **结尾河的比喻**：**能复述"结冰要几小时、有时几天；冰面收住一个完美而水晶般的世界；一颗像单个音节一样落下的小石头就能把它击碎"**，**并说出它同时是"写作的定义"与"写作的危险"。**
8. **lê 的情节骨架（五段）**：**照片里的越南与父母的恋爱 → 出生（铁管、磷光女孩）→ Linda Vista（鱼骨表格、Yang、花园、绿色公寓）→ 剃头／照片／砸碗／"我要做那个流氓" → 阁楼与拆迁。**
9. **"This is in the photograph, though it is not visible to the eye"**：**能说出它同时是**"**后记忆的定义**（照片只给表面，真相在别处）**与**"**写作的宣言**（我要把看不见的那道凹痕写出来）**。**加分点**：接上 Hirsch 的"照片是屏幕"与"预先形成的形象"（"我们盯着这些形象看，而真相在别处"）。**
10. **铁管那一段**：**能说出它至少四重意思（游戏场／孕妇进不去的容器／烧焦的巨人的手臂／棺材）**，**并说明"母亲进不去"这一笔为什么关键**——**她要回的是"小时候睡在里面"的越南，而战争已经把那根管子变成了别的东西。**
11. **"They call us Yang"**：**能把它与 7.1 的 "racial lumping" 直接接上**（**"My brother died in your jungle"**），**并指出年鉴那句"我们不是任何项目里的最多"是"被统计但不被区分"的最佳证据。**
12. **"When I grow up I am going to be the gangster we are all looking for"**：**能解释"gangster"的四层（父亲的出身／母亲的咒骂／父亲的自辩／女儿的宣言）**，**并说明它如何把"污名"改写成"主体位置"**（与 3.1／3.2 的 model minority／yellow peril 逻辑互为反面）。**
13. **阁楼那一段**：**能背 "sometimes you don't need to see or touch people to know they're there"**，**并说明它为什么是全书对 Hirsch 最简洁的一次翻译**（**联结不靠索引性，靠需要**）。
14. **"长城是纸板"**：**能说明它是全篇的"祛魅时刻"**——**父亲的爱（为女儿修一条上后院的捷径）、童年的奇观、非法改建的斜坡，是同一个东西**；**而祛魅并不取消爱，只是把它放回它的物质条件里。**
15. **结尾"血只在我的喉咙里"**：**能说明它把暴力从"现场"转移到"讲述"**；**加分点**：与 Le 的"一个音节击碎冰面"对照——**两篇都把"讲述"当成最后的现场。**
16. **可能的短论题**：
    - **"Le 的叙述者说'如果写真实的故事，更好卖'；他的父亲说'他们要的是怜悯，他们会鼓掌然后忘掉'。"** 讨论：**在一个把族裔创伤当卖点的市场里，"被记住"是否可能？**
    - **"lê 的叙述者说'血只在我的喉咙里'。"** 用**照片、铁管、阁楼**三个意象说明：**"讲述"在这本书里是伤口还是止血带？**
    - **"Hirsch 说后记忆靠家庭、照片与公共档案传递，而它的危险是把历史私有化。"** 用 **Le 的烧稿** 与 **lê 的阁楼** 回答：**哪一种"私有化"更可取？**
    - **"Espiritu 说越南难民的后代拿到的不是记忆，而是'一个任性的档案'。"** 用两个文本举证：**这个"档案"里到底有什么？（一张合影、一个姓、一张名片、一根铁管、一条纸板长城。）**
    - **"Vang 说'东南亚裔美国人'这个范畴被战争造出来。"** 讨论：**Le（越南出生、澳洲长大、在爱荷华写作）与 lê（越南出生、圣地亚哥长大）分别在这张地图上的什么位置？"东南亚裔美国人"能装下他们吗？**
17. **必须记的名字／年份／文本**：**Nam Le／lê thi diem thúy／The Boat（2008）／The Gangster We Are All Looking For（2003）／Iowa Writers' Workshop／My Lai（Tu Cung, Quang Ngai）／Linda Vista, San Diego／nước／"ETHNIC STORY"／"Yang"／Faulkner 的 "old verities"**。
